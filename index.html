DETECTORA PARTY GAME OF LIES

One of them is telling the truth. The rest are lying through their teeth. 🕵️‍♂️

HOW TO LAUNCH

Host mode (TV / laptop): press Host on this screen (or load ?host). A QR code and 4-letter room code appear. 3–8 players needed.

Controller mode (phones): scan the TV's QR code (opens …#ABCD) or press Join as player and type the code.

Each round: everyone votes a trivia domain → one player is the GUESSER and sees no answers. One other player is the TRUTH SPY (real answer, tells the truth); everyone else is a MASTER BLUFF (real answer, invents a believable lie). After 10 seconds of prep, they speak out loud to the Guesser, who gets 2 chances to pick the truth-teller (just 1 chance in a 3-player game).

Scoring: Guesser +200 (1st try) / +100 (2nd) · Truth Spy +100 if detected · Liar +150 each time the Guesser picks them by mistake.

Needs: the page hosted at a normal web address (not file://) so phones can open it.
