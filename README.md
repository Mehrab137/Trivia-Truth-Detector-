# Trivia-Truth-Detector - How to Play
 
**Players:** 3–8 · **Goal:** score the most points over the night.
 
## Each case
1. **Pick a domain (15 s).** Everyone votes for one of 5 trivia domains. The most votes wins.
2. **A hard question appears.** Players take turns being the **Guesser**.
   - **Guesser:** sees the question but *no answers*.
   - **Truth Spy 🕵️‍♂️:** one random player. Sees the real answer and must say it honestly.
   - **Master Bluffs 🎭:** everyone else. They also see the real answer, but must lie convincingly.
   - **Skip:** if more than half the players vote to skip, the question is replaced (max 2 skips per case).
3. **Bluff prep (10 s).** The Spy and the Bluffs get ten seconds to plan their answers.
4. **Interrogation.** Everyone speaks out loud, one at a time, giving their answer and defending it. The Guesser may grill them. Then the Guesser picks who they think is telling the truth.
   - You get **2 chances**.
   - A wrong pick shows a red **DETECTION FAILED** banner, and that player is struck out.
   - A right pick shows a green **TRUTH DETECTED** banner.
   - After 2 misses, the real Truth-Teller is revealed.
## Scoring
| Who | Points |
|---|---|
| Guesser finds the Spy on the 1st try | **+200** |
| Guesser finds the Spy on the 2nd try | **+100** |
| Truth Spy, if detected | **+100** |
| Each Bluff, every time they are wrongly picked | **+150** |
 
The turn passes to the next player, and the player with the most points at the end wins.
 
**Tips:** Bluffs, don't be too confident. Spy, don't act too honest.
